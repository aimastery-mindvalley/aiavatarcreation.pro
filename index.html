How It Works

Four weeks, then you run it.

I work with one expert at a time, and I build your system with you, so at the end you run it without me.

Week 1

Your avatar and your brain

In one working session of about four hours, we build your avatar, your voice and three looks. Then we write down who you help, what you believe and how you speak, so the scripts sound like you.

Week 2

Your strategy and first scripts

We plan what you will post and write the first three scripts together. I write the rest, and you approve them within two business days.

Week 3

Your videos

You generate each approved script in your own account, with the written guide. Then we review, edit, caption and schedule the videos together.

Week 4

The handover

You run the whole process yourself while I am there. You keep the system, the guide and twelve videos scheduled, three a week, for the month after.

Common Questions

Honest answers to real concerns

Experts
"Will people know it is an avatar?"
Yes, because we say so on every video. A good avatar, disclosed, does not cost you clients. What does damage is the hidden one.
"AI doesn't sound like me. I tried."
That happens when the AI was never trained on your material. We start with your posts, your articles and your messages, so the scripts sound like you.
"I'm not technical."
You do not need to be during the build. You learn to run it at the end, with the written guide, and that is the point of the handover.
"I like being on camera."
Then the system still writes your scripts and you shoot them yourself. The avatar is the default, and it is never a requirement.
"What happens when we stop working together?"
You keep the system. That is what you paid for.
Business & Industry
"Can this reflect our company's tone and standards?"
Built on your brand voice, your visual standards, and your approval process. Nothing publishes without your sign-off.
"How much input will our team need to provide?"
The first phase is the heaviest. Defining voice, approvals, and source material together. From there, your team reviews scripts and signs off on outputs. That is the ongoing ask.
"Can you turn our existing documents or slide decks into videos?"
Yes. Your manuals, SOPs, and training decks become the source material. We structure, script, and produce from what you already have.
"Will this work for technical or industry-specific training?"
That is where this works best. Technical content, compliance modules, and specialized training all benefit from clear, repeatable delivery.
"Can we use multiple speakers or languages?"
Yes. Multiple avatar presenters, multiple languages, consistent delivery across every version.
