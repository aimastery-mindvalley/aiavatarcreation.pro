The process

From your documents to a finished video, with your sign-off at every step.

Your technical documentation, manuals, procedures and training packages are the source material. I turn them into clear scripts with your subject matter experts, and your approver signs each one off before production.

1

Your knowledge, collected

We pick the first module and gather the source: the manual, the deck, or a conversation with the person who knows the procedure best.

2

The script, in your tone

Following ADDIE, the standard instructional design model, I write a script that says one thing clearly, in the vernacular of their workplace. Your subject matter expert corrects it, and your approver signs it off.

3

The video, in every language

An avatar presenter delivers the approved script, with captions and your branding, in the languages your teams speak. Delivered as an MP4 for your intranet or controlled document management system, or as a SCORM package for your learning management system.

Common Questions

What clients ask me

Experts
"Will people know it is an avatar?"
Yes, because we say so on every video. When the avatar is clearly disclosed, your clients keep trusting you. Trust breaks only when an avatar is hidden.
"AI doesn't sound like me. I tried."
That happens when the AI was never trained on your material. We start with your posts, your articles and your messages, so the scripts sound like you. And you can always write a script yourself and make the video from it.
"I'm not technical."
You don't need to be. I handle the technical side during the build, and at the end you learn to run the system with a written guide. That's what the handover is for.
"How much of my time does it take?"
About four hours for the build session in week one. After that, a few hours a week to approve the scripts, make the videos and review them with me. Once you run it alone, you set the pace.
"What happens when we stop working together?"
You keep the system. That's what you paid for.
Organizations
"Do we need to film anyone?"
No. An AI avatar presenter delivers the script, so nobody books a studio or a filming day. If you want a manager's own avatar, we make it from their photos and voice, with their consent, in one short session.
"Can this reflect our company's tone and standards?"
Yes. Every video matches your brand voice and your visual standards, and nothing is published until your team signs it off.
"Can you turn our existing documents or slide decks into videos?"
Yes. Your manuals, procedures and training decks become the source material, and I write the scripts and produce the videos from the material you provide.
"What happens when a procedure changes?"
We change the script and re-issue the video. Nobody goes back in front of a camera, and the updated version replaces the old one in your library.
"Who owns the videos?"
You do. Every video is delivered as a file in your name, and the scripts stay with you, so the library keeps working whether or not we keep working together.
