The AI content system

An AI assistant writes your scripts in your voice, or you write your own.

The system is your Content OS: a private workspace in Notion, a popular app for pages, lists and calendars, built around your content. It holds your brand compass (who you help, what you stand for, how you speak), a knowledge hub with your own posts, articles and offers, your content strategy, a library of scripts, and a planner with a date for every video.

An AI assistant, Claude or ChatGPT, whichever you already use, reads all of it before it writes your scripts. Everything is set up in your own accounts, so the system belongs to you and keeps working after we finish.

1

Your business, in your words

Together we fill your brand compass and your knowledge hub: who you help, what you offer, what you believe, and the words you actually use. We add your best posts, your articles and the questions your clients ask, so the assistant writes from your own material instead of generic advice.

2

The AI writes, or you do

When you ask, the assistant reads everything we wrote down about your business and writes the scripts and captions for your month, about your expertise, in your own voice. If you prefer to write some scripts yourself, they go into the same plan.

3

You decide

You read every script, and you fix or remove anything that does not sound like you. The approved scripts are ready to be turned into videos, and then scheduled in your content planner, so you can see what goes out and when.

Common Questions

What clients ask me

Experts
"Will people know it is an avatar?"
Yes, because we say so on every video. When the avatar is clearly disclosed, your clients keep trusting you. Trust breaks only when an avatar is hidden.
"AI doesn't sound like me. I tried."
That happens when the AI was never trained on your material. We start with your posts, your articles and your messages, so the scripts sound like you. And you can always write a script yourself and make the video from it.
"I'm not technical."
You don't need to be. I handle the technical side during the build, and at the end you learn to run the system with a written guide. That's what the handover is for.
"I like being on camera."
In that case, the system still writes your scripts, and you film the videos yourself. The avatar is the default, but it's never a requirement.
"How much of my time does it take?"
About four hours for the build session in week one. After that, a few hours a week to approve the scripts, make the videos and review them with me. Once you run it alone, you set the pace.
"What do I need before we start?"
Paying clients, so the videos have something to sell. A few posts, articles or messages written in your own words. And four hours for the first session, where we build your avatar and your voice together.
"What happens when we stop working together?"
You keep the system. That's what you paid for.
Business & Industry
"Can this reflect our company's tone and standards?"
Yes. Every video matches your brand voice and your visual standards, and nothing is published until your team signs it off.
"How much input will our team need to provide?"
The first few weeks require the most input, because together we define your brand voice, who approves what, and the source material. After that, your team reviews the scripts and approves the final videos.
"Can you turn our existing documents or slide decks into videos?"
Yes. Your manuals, procedures and training decks become the source material, and I write the scripts and produce the videos from the material you provide.
"Will this work for technical or industry-specific training?"
Yes, this is where it works best. Technical content, compliance modules and specialized training are easier to follow when the delivery is clear and consistent.
"Can we use multiple speakers or languages?"
Yes. You can use several avatar presenters and produce videos in multiple languages, with the same quality in every version.
